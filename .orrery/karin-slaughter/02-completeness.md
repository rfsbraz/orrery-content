## Correction that reorders the derived order

**Cold, Cold Heart: 2016 -> 2011.** The author's own page states plainly
it "was previously in First Thrills [Volume 3]... and now available on
its own"; Google Books' table of contents for that anthology confirms her
contribution is titled "Cold Cold Heart" (Forge Books, 7 Jun 2011). The
2016 date the wing carried was the standalone reissue, exactly the
edition-that-sold bias the dating rule warns about. This moves the work
from between The Kept Woman (2016) and Last Breath (2017) to the 2011
cluster, alongside Fallen, The Unremarkable Heart and Other Stories, and
Thorn in My Side.

## Rejected

- **The Mystery Writers of America Cookbook** (2015): a 100+ contributor
  charity cookbook; Slaughter's piece is a recipe with a short bio note,
  not narrative fiction. Fails the bar for a fiction bibliography the same
  way a blurb or foreword would.
- **Deadly Anniversaries** (2020, MWA anthology): reprints The Unremarkable
  Heart verbatim. No new content, so no new entry.
- **Root Bound** (2004): Slaughter's opening Like a Charm chapter. Unlike
  its closing-chapter twin, no independent ebook/ISBN listing was found
  anywhere; it stays folded into the Like a Charm entry rather than
  manufacturing a standalone record for it.
- **First Thrills: Volume 3** itself: not a second entry. Her only
  contribution to it is Cold, Cold Heart (above); the anthology's other
  four contributors are unrelated authors outside this wing.

   next reviews this wing's shelf/order presentation.

   what is listed here; that is a documented negative, not a completed
   claim of exhaustiveness for a 20+ year career this heavily anthologized.
